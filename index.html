About Us

Longxiang Communication Equipment Co., Ltd. Founded on April 20, 2015, rooted in Chongqing, China, has grown into a leading modern enterprise integrating R&D, precision manufacturing, brand marketing, and global supply chain trade.

Since inception, we uphold 'quality-driven innovation, integrity forging the future'. We provide comprehensive precision communication accessories including power charging, cables, audio, and car mounts. Backed by automated production lines and strict QC, our products are exported globally.

We invest heavily in R&D for fast charging, magnetic wireless tech, and acoustic noise reduction. All products pass ISO9001, CE, FCC, and RoHS certifications.

Looking forward, Longxiang Communication will continue to uphold the spirit of 'win-win cooperation and excellence', deepening our global strategy. We warmly welcome clients worldwide to cooperate with us.

Return & Exchange Policy

1. 7-Day Return Guarantee & Conditions
To protect customers' rights, all 3C digital accessories purchased enjoy a 7-day unconditional return service from the date of receipt, provided the items are unopened, unused, and in original packaging.
2. Quality Issues & Non-Artificial Defects
If you find product performance failures or non-human defects within 7 days, please contact customer service with photos/videos. After verification, we will replace it with a new one and cover the freight.
3. Exceptions Where Returns Are Not Accepted
The following cases are not eligible for returns: (1) Over 7 days or warranty expired; (2) Counterfeit products; (3) Human-made damage due to misuse, water ingress, dropping, unauthorized disassembly; (4) Custom or clearance items.
4. Refund Process, Timeline & Channels
Once your return is approved and received, we will inspect and process the refund within 3-5 business days. Funds will be returned to your original payment account within 5-7 business days.
